# BeatNote Support

BeatNote is a local music annotation and rehearsal app for dancers and choreographers.

For questions or bug reports, contact [gswm98@gmail.com](mailto:gswm98@gmail.com) or join the
[BeatNote Helpdesk on Telegram](https://t.me/+4edtI3WUOqZlN2U1). Email is available if you do not use Telegram.

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the BeatNote version/build shown in Settings →
About BeatNote, and the steps that caused the issue. A screenshot can help. Avoid posting private
audio, personal information or confidential projects in the group.

## Keep your work safe

Keep original audio files and export important projects before deleting app data or uninstalling.
BeatNote V1 stores audio and projects locally and does not offer cloud sync. Reinstalling after
deleting the app does not restore its local library.

For playback problems, try the app's Reload Audio action if offered and include the error text in
your report. For import problems, include the file format and approximate song length; you do not
need to send the audio itself.

Read the [BeatNote Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for storage, sharing and support-message details.
